# Nuclear Splitting Shows Binding Energy

## Example

The splitting of the nucleus shows that a highly constrained stable object can contain deep energetic binding.

## Why It Matters

It distinguishes two senses of energy:

- the energy needed to maintain a stable state
- the energy involved in the binding relations of that state

A nucleus can be stable under ordinary conditions while still releasing enormous energy when its binding relation is changed.

## Pattern

High constraint can create a stable low-maintenance form. But the constraints themselves can be energetically deep. When those constraints are broken or rearranged, the energy difference becomes available.
