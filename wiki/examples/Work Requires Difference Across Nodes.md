# Work Requires Difference Across Nodes

## Example

A heat engine does not work if the environment is the same temperature as the heat engine. Electrons do not travel as current without a difference across nodes. Usable energy requires difference.

## Why It Matters

This grounds the claim that pure potential contains no usable energy until relation/difference exists. A uniform thermal state may contain energy, but it cannot do work without a gradient. A circuit may contain charge carriers, but electron flow requires voltage difference across nodes. Relation creates usable structure.

## Pattern

The heat engine works because there is a relation between hot and cold. A dynamo likewise depends on structured relation and motion: fields, conductors, rotation, and difference. Electron current requires difference across nodes. Without relation, difference, and constraint, potential does not become usable work.

This supports the distinction:

- Pure potential: all potential energy possible, but no usable energy.
- Relation/constraint: difference appears.
- Actual information: this in relation to that.
- Usable energy/work: gradients can be exploited.
