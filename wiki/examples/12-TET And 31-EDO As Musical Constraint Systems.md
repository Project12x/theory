# 12-TET And 31-EDO As Musical Constraint Systems

## Example

Comparing 12-TET and 31-EDO shows how music creates meaning through constraint.

## Why It Matters

Both tuning systems divide pitch space into a finite symbolic grid. They do not merely provide more or fewer notes. They define what intervals are available, what relationships are easy to hear, what harmonic moves feel stable, and what kinds of musical language can become fluent.

12-TET strongly constrains pitch space into a compact, highly portable system. It sacrifices many microtonal distinctions in exchange for modulation, instrument standardization, and shared musical infrastructure.

31-EDO constrains pitch space differently. It preserves more interval distinctions and gives access to relationships that 12-TET compresses away, but it also requires different listening habits, notation, instruments, and symbolic fluency.

## Pattern

Music does not become meaningful by maximizing pitch possibility. It becomes meaningful by choosing a constraint system. The constraint grid determines what sonic relationships can be stabilized, repeated, taught, remembered, and emotionally understood.

12-TET and 31-EDO are therefore not just alternate tunings. They are alternate symbolic worlds for organizing sonic possibility.

As the tuning system constrains pitch space, it can extract more usable meaning even though it reduces raw pitch possibility. But every grid has a resolution limit. 12-TET gains portability and shared harmonic infrastructure by compressing distinctions. 31-EDO preserves more distinctions, extracting different meanings, but also demands more resolution from performer, instrument, notation, and listener.

At the other extreme, combining many tones densely enough can return the result toward white noise. Music lives between unconstrained sonic possibility and over-dense collapse of distinction.
