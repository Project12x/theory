# Collapse From Pure Probability

## Definition

The literal operation by which pure potential is constrained through relation into actual information.

## Explanation

Collapse is not merely an analogy across levels. It is one operation appearing at different scales. Constraint is collapse. Relation is required for constraint. Nothing can be constrained without relation.

At the symbolic level, possibility collapses into transmissible signal. At the consciousness level, undifferentiated process collapses into self and not-self. At the cosmological level, pre-geometric potential collapses into relational structure. These are scale-specific appearances of the same operation.
