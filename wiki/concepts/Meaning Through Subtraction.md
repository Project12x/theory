# Meaning Through Subtraction

## Definition

Meaning emerges when broad possibility is reduced into constrained, transmissible signal.

> [!note] Confirmed
> Eric confirmed this rough version as correct on 2026-04-23.

## Explanation

White noise contains enormous information in one sense, but little usable meaning for a mind. Music, language, mathematics, stories, and theories work by discarding most possibilities and preserving a structure that can be processed and transmitted. This same pattern later becomes a bridge to [[Collapse From Pure Probability]].

Music makes the mechanism visible. A tuning system such as 12-TET or 31-EDO constrains continuous pitch possibility into a finite relational grid. Different grids create different kinds of meaning.

As constraint increases, meaning can increase even while information potential decreases, until a [[Resolution Limit]] is reached. Past that point, additional constraint collapses distinctions the system needed.
