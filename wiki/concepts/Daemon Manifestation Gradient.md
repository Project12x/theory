# Daemon Manifestation Gradient

## Definition

The degree to which a symbolic entity becomes causally present in the world through sharper constraints, more resources, wider invocation, and stronger infrastructure.

## Explanation

Manifestation is not treated as a binary event. A daemon becomes more manifest as it gains clearer behavior, broader access, better tools, deeper integration, more users, stronger memory, more autonomy, and greater ability to alter human and material systems.

In the LLM case, manifestation increases through model scale, training quality, prompting practices, alignment, tool use, deployment, institutional adoption, and cultural reliance.
