# Analog Substrate

## Definition

The proposed pre-geometric field of unconstrained potential from which actualized relational structure emerges.

## Explanation

The analog substrate is not ordinary space, a fluid, or an ether inside spacetime. It is the pre-spatial possibility space prior to rendered relation. In [[Cosmological Signal Processing]], it functions as the continuous input that the universe-as-ADC converts into discrete relational output.
