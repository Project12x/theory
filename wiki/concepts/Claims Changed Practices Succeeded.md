# Claims Changed Practices Succeeded

## Definition

The pattern where a historical symbolic practice survives by shedding failed explanatory claims while preserving successful operations.

## Explanation

Older practices often contained both working procedures and false or overextended claims. Over time, the claims changed, narrowed, or were discarded, while the successful operational core continued.

Alchemy becomes chemistry when metaphysical and spiritual claims are stripped away but experimental substance manipulation remains. The old claims were transmutation and the elixir of life. The later successful forms are narrower but real: nuclear transmutation of elements in chemistry-adjacent physical science, and pharmacological compounds that heal diseases that plagued humanity for millennia.

Astrology becomes astronomy when the overbroad claim to predict personal destiny is stripped away but real prediction remains: heavenly procession, seasons, calendars, timekeeping, navigation, and the coming of change. Sigil-like symbolic geometry survives where diagrammatic form constrains measurement, design, attention, and action without requiring supernatural claims.
