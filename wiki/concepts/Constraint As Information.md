# Constraint As Information

## Definition

Information increases as possibility is constrained into more specific, actionable structure.

Information begins at first relation: this in relation to that.

## Explanation

A box with no constraints is barely known. Each additional constraint, such as brown, square, cardboard, hollow, or portable, reduces uncertainty and increases usable information. Consciousness builds practical reality by applying and manipulating such constraints.

At the physical level, a system reads a constraint by interacting with it. Chloroplast pigments pattern-match with specific wavelengths of sunlight. This is reading as physical interaction, not cognition.

Constraint is collapse. A possibility becomes information when relation constrains it into actual form. Nothing can be constrained without relation.

Highly constrained forms can also be energetically bound. Low thermodynamic maintenance energy does not mean the constraint lacks binding energy.
