# Resolution Limit

## Definition

The point at which additional constraint no longer extracts more usable meaning and instead begins collapsing meaningful distinctions.

## Explanation

Constraint creates meaning by reducing possibility into usable structure. But a system can only compress so far before it loses distinctions that matter. That threshold is the resolution limit.

In music, a tuning system has a resolution limit: it can make certain pitch relationships clear while hiding or compressing others. In cognition, categories help perception until they become too coarse. In symbolic systems, names and models clarify until they flatten what they need to preserve.

Combining tones can return to white noise: a direct example of structure crossing a density threshold and losing the distinctions that made it meaningful.

In the cosmology layer, black holes are treated as a speculative extreme case: a region becomes so highly constrained by information and pattern that it begins to function like a pre-information state of pure potential.
