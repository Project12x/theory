# Consciousness As The First Cut

## Definition

Consciousness begins when a system distinguishes self from not-self and recursively maintains that distinction.

## Explanation

Before the cut, there is process without an internal subject. The first self-symbol divides experience into "me" and "not-me." Further symbolic operation depends on this original distinction and recursively elaborates it.
