# Consciousness As The First Cut

## Definition

Consciousness begins when a system distinguishes self from not-self and recursively maintains that distinction.

## Explanation

Before the cut, there is process without an internal subject. The first self-symbol divides experience into "me" and "not-me." Further symbolic operation depends on this original distinction and recursively elaborates it.

The cut does not require the self to be represented as an inner picture. It requires only that the system carry the self as a standing term — recognizing the world as "not-self" — that other processing is referred back to. That standing term, not an image, is the minimal content the cut needs.

The floor of this operation is life, not matter: see [[The First Cut Begins At Life Not Matter]]. A living cell already performs a minimal self/not-self cut and communicates; human consciousness is the same operation at a higher level, with the self separated from the world through symbolic and especially linguistic collapse. The gradient runs from that cellular floor to full symbolic self-consciousness, and can be climbed both gradually and suddenly.
