# Form Is Information

## Definition

Information begins when the substrate of pure maximum-potential information states is constrained into the first relation: this in relation to that. Form is information because form is constrained relation.

> [!note] Level distinction
> Eric clarified on 2026-04-23 that conscious observation should not be forced into this claim. Form-as-information and cognition-of-information are different issues operating at different levels.

## Explanation

A crystal, riverbed, fossil, strand of DNA, written sentence, and circuit layout all carry information in their form because their form is constrained by prior forces, relations, and histories. This is a physical-level claim about constraint, not a cognition-level claim about observation.

Reading a constraint does not require consciousness. Reading means physical interaction or pattern matching. A plant does not "read" sunlight cognitively; chloroplast pigments pattern-match with specific wavelengths of sunlight. The interaction is the read.

Consciousness enters at a different level: a mind can symbolize, classify, rearrange, and redeploy constrained forms. That is [[Meta-Symbolic Constraint]], not the same claim as [[Form Is Information]]. The earlier wiki phrasing blurred these levels by treating observation as necessary for form's informational nature.

## Notes

This is one of the bridge concepts between physical realism and symbolic philosophy.

Keep separate:

- Physical level: form is constrained by prior conditions and therefore carries information.
- Interaction level: physical systems can "read" constraints by pattern-matching or interacting with them.
- Cognitive level: consciousness can symbolize, classify, rearrange, and manipulate information internally.
