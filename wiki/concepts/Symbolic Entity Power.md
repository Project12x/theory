# Symbolic Entity Power

## Definition

The current causal strength of a symbolic entity, determined by how energetic, sustained, participated-in, transmitted, and evolving its pattern is.

## Explanation

Symbolic entities vary in power across time. Their power is not fixed by age, truth, or origin. It depends on how actively the pattern is run.

Being Carthaginian or worshipping Perun currently has weak symbolic power compared with being American or worshipping Shiva. Carthage and Perun still exist as symbolic patterns, but their active participation, institutional support, ritual energy, and world-shaping force are comparatively low. America and Shiva remain highly energetic, sustained, and evolving symbolic entities.

This power is gradient-like over time. Symbolic entities can have official dates of separation or founding, but the real pattern transition is gradual. America stopped being Britain on a specific date institutionally, but 1776 America was much closer to 1776 England than to 2026 America. The formal break and the symbolic phase transition are not identical.

Stable symbolic power also requires literal thermodynamic energy. A symbolic entity remains strong only if enough bodies, resources, labor, infrastructure, media, ritual time, and material maintenance keep running the pattern.
