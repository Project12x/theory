# Quantization Noise Floor

## Definition

The proposed minimum stable resolution at which analog potential can be rendered into discrete actual structure, identified speculatively with `h`.

## Explanation

In [[Cosmological Signal Processing]], Planck's constant is interpreted as a quantization floor: the minimum threshold required to resolve potential into stable actual states. This pairs with [[Relational Update Rate]] to form the signal-processing analogy.
