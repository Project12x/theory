# Primordial Power Taming

## Definition

The process by which consciousness constrains natural powers into repeatable, usable causal systems.

## Explanation

Technology does not create power from nowhere. It identifies, constrains, channels, and stabilizes forces that precede human intention: fire, water, pressure, electricity, magnetism, combustion, radiation, fossil sunlight, and elements forged in stars. These powers become "technology" when they are symbolically understood, mechanically constrained, and made repeatable.

This is why technology can be understood as empirical magic. Fantasy magitech often imagines machines powered by magic. The claim here is that our world already does this with natural forces: we bind primordial processes into artifacts that light homes, move bodies, preserve life, and compute symbols.

Humans tame winds, waves, and star-born matter. We create miniature versions of vast natural powers and make them obey: contained fire, pressure systems, electrical grids, magnetic fields, engines, turbines, batteries, antennas, and invisible tempests of magnetism. We harness these forces across thousands of miles to perform will-like functions at a distance.
