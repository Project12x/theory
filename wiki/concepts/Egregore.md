# Egregore

## Definition

A group-generated distributed symbolic entity sustained by shared attention, ritual, repetition, participation, and symbolic reinforcement.

## Explanation

An egregore is real in the same sense other symbolic entities are real: not as a supernatural being outside the world, but as a physically instantiated thought-pattern distributed across brains, bodies, language, ritual, institutions, and media.

Belief can strengthen an egregore, but explicit belief is not required for its persistence. Repeated patterned participation can sustain it even when many participants do not understand the structure they are helping maintain.

In this framework, egregore is best treated as a scale or resolution term rather than a separate ontological category. What occult traditions call an egregore is usually a group-scale manifestation of the broader class of distributed symbolic entities.

This means selves, fandoms, movements, institutions, nations, and gods can all be egregore-like at different scales. What changes is not the basic structure, but the amount of thermodynamic investment, symbolic density, persistence, infrastructure, and causal reach.

The god of a large religious civilization can therefore be understood as a civilization-scale egregore: a highly energized symbolic entity maintained across many hosts and forms of physical infrastructure.
