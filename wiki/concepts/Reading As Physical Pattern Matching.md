# Reading As Physical Pattern Matching

## Definition

Reading a constraint means physically interacting with it through pattern match, not necessarily consciously interpreting it.

## Explanation

In this framework, "reading" starts below cognition. A plant does not read sunlight in a mental sense. Chloroplast pigments pattern-match with specific wavelengths, and that physical interaction extracts usable structure from the environment. The interaction is the read.

Conscious reading is a higher-level case where the physical interaction is further symbolized, classified, and manipulated internally.
