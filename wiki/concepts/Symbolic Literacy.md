# Symbolic Literacy

## Definition

The ability to perceive, evaluate, and manipulate symbolic systems as real causal structures rather than as mere stories, decorations, or beliefs.

## Explanation

Symbolic literacy is what lets someone see the continuity between old magical practices and modern technical systems. It requires holding multiple levels at once: claim, practice, effect, transmission, recategorization, and infrastructure.

Without symbolic literacy, people tend to see modern categories as clean breaks: magic versus technology, superstition versus science, myth versus fact. With symbolic literacy, those categories become historical edits over an unbroken operational chain.
