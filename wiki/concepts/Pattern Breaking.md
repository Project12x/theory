# Pattern Breaking

## Definition

The disruption, revision, or destabilization of an existing symbolic/self pattern.

## Explanation

Pattern breaking occurs when social pressure, trauma, isolation, new information, ritual, altered states, or internal symbolic reorganization disrupt an established pattern. It can be destructive, liberating, destabilizing, clarifying, or transformative depending on what breaks and what replaces it.

In the self model, pattern breaking is one of the forces that changes identity over time.
