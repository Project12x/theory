# Foam Across Scales

## Definition

"Foam" names three related but distinct structural levels in this ontology: quantum foam, cosmic foam, and metaverse foam.

## Explanation

The same word should not collapse three different claims into one.

### Quantum Foam

At the smallest scale, quantum structure is not perfectly smooth. There is real fine-grained instability, discreteness, or fluctuation in the actualized relational lattice.

### Cosmic Foam

At universe scale, matter and structure arrange into a foam-like pattern: walls, filaments, nodes, voids, and horizon-bounded large-scale structure. This is the foam-like structure most directly visible within our universe.

### Metaverse Foam

Beyond any one universe, the broader ontology is also foam-like: multiple noninteracting universe-lattices or horizon-bounded structures can exist without sharing ordinary space. This is not a bigger container full of touching bubbles. It is a broader pattern of separate actualizations within unconstrained pure possibility.

These three are related by reflection across scale, but they are not interchangeable. Quantum foam concerns fine structure. Cosmic foam concerns large-scale universe structure. Metaverse foam concerns the broader ontological pattern in which separate universes can exist without meaningful shared space.

## Implications

- "Foam" in this system is a multiscale structural intuition, not one single physics claim.
- Quantum foam, cosmic foam, and metaverse foam can all exist at once.
- The universe can be foam-like at scale without reducing the claim to Wheeler-style quantum foam alone.
