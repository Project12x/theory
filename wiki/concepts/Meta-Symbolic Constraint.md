# Meta-Symbolic Constraint

## Definition

An internal symbolic structure that constrains how reality is perceived, modeled, recombined, and later manipulated.

## Explanation

Meta-symbols are not just marks, words, or external signs. They are internalized constraint patterns. A mind does not merely see a box; it constrains it through properties, uses, relations, and possible transformations. Those constraints become manipulable internally before they become manipulable externally.
