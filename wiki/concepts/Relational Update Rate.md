# Relational Update Rate

## Definition

The finite rate at which pure potential becomes actual information across the Planck length.

## Explanation

In the cosmological layer, `c` is interpreted as the operational rate of relational establishment: the causal slew rate of the universe's rendering process. Eric refined this as the rate at which pure potential is turned into actual information across the Planck length. This reframes the speed of light as a Planck-scale information actualization limit rather than merely a velocity through pre-existing space.

Energy may be downstream or related, because pure potential contains no usable energy but contains all potential energy possible. Information/relation comes first in the definition.

The rate is linked to the primordial crystal lattice, which sets both the Planck-length relation scale and the maximum causal update rate.
