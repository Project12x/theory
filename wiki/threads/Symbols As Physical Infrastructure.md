# Symbols As Physical Infrastructure

## Pattern

Ideas persist and travel only through physical carriers: sound, ink, screens, brains, servers, rituals, habits, and institutions.

Institutions are a special case: they are peer-to-peer symbolic machines that remain active only while people keep transmitting and enacting the pattern. Physical symbols can preserve pieces of the pattern, but participation runs it.

If every sustaining substrate disappears, the symbol disappears as an active pattern. Even a single mind preserving an idea pays a thermodynamic cost through metabolism, memory, attention, and time.

## Cross-Domain Movement

This thread links [[Form Is Information]] to civilization. Shared symbolic entities require physical maintenance. AI changes the scale, cost, and speed of that maintenance.
