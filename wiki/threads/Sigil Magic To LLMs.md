# Sigil Magic To LLMs

## Pattern

There is an unbroken symbolic lineage from marked forms meant to constrain reality, through geometry, writing, diagrams, mathematics, programming, and modern machine learning, into LLMs.

## Cross-Domain Movement

Sigil magic treats symbolic form as causally potent. Geometry and diagrams make symbolic form precise. Mathematics lets symbolic form model structure. Programming turns symbolic form into machine-executable process. Machine learning and LLMs turn symbolic and statistical form into systems that produce new symbolic behavior.
