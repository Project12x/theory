# The Unbroken Line

## Summary

The main philosophical spine of the repo. It argues that human history is one continuous escalation of [[Empirical Magic]]: symbolic manipulation producing real effects in physical reality. It begins with symbols, magic, technology, religion, institutions, and AI, then extends into [[Consciousness As The First Cut]] and finally into [[Collapse Cosmology]].

This source contains Eric's original authorial ideas, but Eric clarified on 2026-04-23 that AI was used to organize thoughts and sometimes injected extra postulation or personalization he did not intend. Treat extracted claims as needing confirmation unless they are explicitly owned elsewhere.

> [!warning] Claim extraction warning
> Do not automatically treat every strong claim in this source as Eric's settled position. Some phrasing or speculative extensions may be AI-organized or AI-injected draft material.

## Core Contributions

- Defines working magic as symbolically mediated causation rather than supernatural fantasy.
- Treats symbols as physical constraints that transmit information across minds, time, and distance.
- Frames meaning as the result of subtraction, filtering, and compression.
- Reinterprets gods, institutions, moral systems, and stories as symbolic entities running across distributed human networks.
- Treats AI as the newest substrate for symbolic production and possible symbolic agency.
- Extends the same subtractive operation into consciousness and cosmology.
