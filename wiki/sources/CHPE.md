# CHPE

## Summary

CHPE, or Crystallized Hyperdimensional Probability Emergence, is the repo's current physics-facing formalization branch. It attempts to model emergent physics through labeled hypergraph configurations, local rewriting operators, path sums, observables, correspondence limits, and computational tests.

This document is AI-assisted formalization: the mathematical structure was largely worked out by AI based on Eric's conceptual direction. It should be treated as a provisional formal branch, not as equally authorial with [[The Unbroken Line]] or [[Cosmological Signal Processing]].

## Core Contributions

- Defines states over admissible labeled hypergraph configurations.
- Introduces local rewrite operators with gauge covariance, spin closure, and locality.
- Separates quantum and stochastic evolution modes.
- Defines observables such as graph statistics, entropy, spectral dimension, horizons, and particle-like motifs.
- States QFT and GR correspondence limits.
- Proposes a minimal falsifiable rule set `M0`.
- Lists proof obligations and numerical tests.
