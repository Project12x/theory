# Closest Neighbors To The Relational-Geometry Thesis

## Why It Matters

This note maps the closest intellectual neighbors to the thesis that reality is fundamentally relational, that geometry is not downstream decoration but core ontology, and that many apparent objects or forces are projections of deeper relational structure.

It should be read alongside [[Major Physics Frameworks Are Resolution-Dependent Partial Truths]].

## Very Close

### Wolfram Physics Project

Probably the closest currently visible technical neighbor.

- Reality modeled as evolving hypergraph relations
- No intrinsic space
- No intrinsic time; time corresponds to updates/events
- Matter as stable local hypergraph features
- Causal structure built from update dependence

Main mismatch: Wolfram usually treats the substrate as fundamentally discrete, while this framework insists the deepest substrate is analog and discreteness is emergent.

Source: [Wolfram Physics Project - Basic Concepts](https://www.wolframphysics.org/technical-introduction/potential-relation-to-physics/basic-concepts/), [Time and Spacetime](https://www.wolframphysics.org/technical-introduction/potential-relation-to-physics/time-and-spacetime/)

### Wheeler's Geometrodynamics

Very close in ambition and taste.

- Physics reduced to geometry
- "Mass without mass, charge without charge"
- Matterlike behavior emerging from geometric structure
- Search for unification by treating geometry as fundamental

Main mismatch: Wheeler's framework is still closer to spacetime geometry itself than to an analog substrate plus hypergraph-style collapse process.

Source: [Geometrodynamics overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometrodynamics), [Open Library entry for Wheeler's *Geometrodynamics*](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL2738192W/Geometrodynamics)

### Relational / Structural Cluster

Taken together, [[Relational Quantum Mechanics]]-style thinking plus ontic structural realism are very close to the thesis that relation is more fundamental than isolated substance.

- Facts exist in interactions or relations
- Structure may be more fundamental than things
- Physics may tell us more about relations than intrinsic natures

Main mismatch: these are usually ontological or interpretive positions, not full cosmological mechanisms.

Sources: [SEP - Relational Quantum Mechanics](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/win2016/entries/qm-relational/), [SEP - Structural Realism](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/win2020/entries/structural-realism/)

## Partial

### Leibnizian Relationalism

Very important ancestry.

- Space as order of relations, not a container
- Strong anti-absolute-space impulse

Main mismatch: not a modern physics mechanism, no hypergraph/collapse/computation story.

Source: [SEP - Absolute and Relational Space and Motion](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/spacetime-theories-classical/)

### Mach / Barbour / Shape Dynamics

Strong partial overlap.

- Deep relational critique of absolute space and time
- Shape and relation more fundamental than external background
- Serious rethinking of time

Main mismatch: primarily about gravity/shape/relational dynamics, not a full hypergraph, dark-sector, black-hole, or symbolic-collapse ontology.

Sources: [Barbour - *Shape Dynamics: An Introduction*](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/51889741_Shape_Dynamics_An_Introduction), [Shape Dynamics overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shape_dynamics)

### Loop Quantum Gravity / Penrose Spin-Network Lineage

Strong partial overlap.

- Geometry emerges from combinatorial relational structures
- Planck-scale structure is graph-like
- Space is not fundamentally smooth

Main mismatch: the framework here is more explicitly analog-substrate-first, higher-dimensional, and cosmologically recursive than standard LQG.

Sources: [Rovelli - *Loop Quantum Gravity*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.12942/lrr-2008-5), [Rovelli & Smolin 1995](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.52.5743)

### Kaluza-Klein and Higher-Dimensional Unification

Strong partial overlap on one key axis.

- Gravity and electromagnetism as geometry
- Extra dimension(s) used for unification

Main mismatch: Kaluza-Klein is a specific dimensional-unification move, not a general claim that all 3D reality is projection of deeper relational structure.

Source: [Kaluza-Klein theory overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaluza%E2%80%93Klein_theory)

## Misleadingly Similar

### Generic "Quantum Foam" / "Spacetime Foam" Talk

Can sound close because of shared language about foam and deep structure.

Why misleading:

- Usually concerns Planck-scale fluctuations of spacetime
- Does not by itself imply the broader relational-geometry ontology
- Does not capture the cosmic-foam and metaverse-foam distinctions used here

This is neighbor language, not necessarily neighbor structure.

### Generic Informational Physics / "It From Bit" Slogans

Can sound close because this framework also cares about information.

Why misleading:

- Many information-first approaches do not specify geometry strongly enough
- Many are weaker on relation, force, or cosmological boundary structure
- "Information" by itself is too broad to identify the thesis

Information alone is not yet relational-geometry ontology.

### String Theory As A Whole

Shares extra dimensions, unification ambition, and geometric richness, so it is often the first thing people think of.

Why misleading:

- The thesis here is not built around strings, branes, or perturbative string machinery
- This framework is more relation-first and projection-first
- Time, collapse, horizon structure, and hypergraph-like rendering are doing very different work here

String theory may overlap on some axes, but it is not the nearest family resemblance.

## Current Summary

The nearest neighbors are not one school but a cluster:

- Leibniz / Mach / Barbour on relation over container
- Wheeler on geometry as physics
- Rovelli / structural realism on relations over intrinsic objects
- LQG / Penrose / Wolfram on graph-like or combinatorial structure
- Kaluza-Klein on higher-dimensional geometric unification

What is distinctive here is the attempt to make all of these one continuous ontology spanning physics, cosmology, symbolic structure, and consciousness.
