# Which Unsolved Physics Problems Does The Framework Reframe

## Why It Matters

The framework makes broad ontological claims. A useful test is whether those claims merely rename mysteries or actually reduce the number of distinct unsolved problems by making some of them structurally unsurprising.

Reference checklist: [List of unsolved problems in physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_unsolved_problems_in_physics).

This question should be read together with [[Major Physics Frameworks Are Resolution-Dependent Partial Truths]], since part of the method here is asking whether different successful theories are describing different resolution-layers of the same deeper mechanism.

## Problems The Framework Most Directly Reframes

### Problem of Time

This is one of the strongest apparent fits. If [[Time Is The Causal Tick Of Collapse]] is true, then time is not a dimension in the same sense as space. It is the ordered causal update process. That does not mathematically solve the physics problem, but it gives a direct ontological answer to why quantum mechanics and general relativity may be talking past each other about time.

### Emergence of Spacetime / Quantum Spacetime

The framework directly answers this in spirit: space is rendered relation, not a fundamental container. The three classical dimensions map stable knot-pattern positions on a hypergraph, while time is update order rather than a similar coordinate. See [[Three Classical Dimensions Map Hypergraph Knot Positions]] and [[Time Is The Causal Tick Of Collapse]].

### Black Hole Information / Interior Structure

The framework has a strong answer here. It says the black hole interior is not an ordinary in-universe region; only the event horizon exists in our universe, and the black hole is the other-side structure of the same boundary. If true, this would radically reframe what "information loss" even means. See [[Only The Event Horizon Exists In Our Universe]] and [[Universe And Black Holes Share One Structure]].

### Dark Energy and Dark Matter

The framework gives a unified answer: dark energy and dark matter are not separate fundamental substances, but two observable faces of frontier actualization. Dark matter is more specifically higher-dimensional relation measured in 3D. See [[Dark Energy And Dark Matter Are Frontier Actualization Effects]].

### Extra Dimensions

The framework strongly prefers "yes", but not in a simple string-theory-compactification sense. Higher dimensions are treated as real relational structure whose effects are projected into 3D, showing up in entanglement and dark matter. See [[Entanglement Is Higher-Dimensional Connection Measured In 3D]].

### Horizon Problem / Inflation Family

The framework partially reframes this by treating early-universe collapse as a global or nonlocal relation-establishment event rather than an ordinary local expansion through background space. It therefore reduces the mystery of large-scale homogeneity, though it does not yet provide a formal inflation replacement.

### Neutrino Masses

The framework does not solve neutrino masses yet, but it may have the beginnings of a mechanism. The current best-fit intuition is that neutrinos combine weak 3D lattice binding, higher-dimensional leakage, and oscillation between nearby stable knot-modes. See [[How Could Neutrino Masses Arise In The Framework]].

### Theory of Everything

At the philosophical level, the framework claims a single operation underlies multiple domains: [[Collapse Constraint And Relation Are One Operation]]. That is a serious unification claim, though not yet a mathematical Theory of Everything in the physicist's sense.

## Problems The Framework Partially Touches

### Dimensionless Constants

The framework has a strong story for why `c` and `h` are not arbitrary within a stable causal universe, but that does not yet amount to a derivation of the full set of dimensionless constants.

### Holographic Principle

The horizon-first ontology and information-boundary emphasis rhyme strongly with holographic thinking. The framework may be naturally holographic in spirit, but that is not yet a demonstrated lower-dimensional duality.

### Origin and Future of the Universe

The framework clearly has an answer about origin: unconstrained potential collapses into relational structure. It also has a distinctive answer about future: no truly final stable state exists while the horizon continues to expand. See [[No Truly Stable State Exists While The Horizon Expands]]. What remains open is how to connect that story to current observational cosmology in a disciplined way.

## Problems The Framework Does Not Yet Obviously Solve

- Yang-Mills existence / mass gap
- Rigorous interacting QFT in 4D
- Matter-antimatter asymmetry
- Strong CP problem
- Hierarchy problem in its technical field-theory sense
- Generations of matter
- Muon g-2
- Neutron lifetime
- Exotic hadron classification

These may eventually be touched if a formal hypergraph dynamics is built out, but right now the framework mostly offers ontology, not calculational machinery, for these questions.

## Current Assessment

If the framework is true, several famous unsolved problems become less mysterious at the conceptual level:

- problem of time
- emergence of spacetime
- black-hole interior / information framing
- dark matter and dark energy
- role of extra dimensions

The framework is weakest where physics demands precise quantitative derivation rather than ontological reinterpretation.
