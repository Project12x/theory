# Open Questions And Term Refinement Register

## Why It Matters

The system now has enough internal structure that its weak points are often not missing ideas, but terms whose registers need sharper separation.

This page tracks open questions and terms needing refinement across two broad spheres:

- natural philosophy: Eric's physics-facing ontology of hypergraph crystallization, not a technical physics theory
- general philosophy: symbolic operation, consciousness, civilization, AI, morality, and distributed symbolic entities

The goal is to keep the philosophy bold without letting terms drift between ontology, metaphor, technical physics, social theory, and rhetoric unnoticed.

## Current State

Eric clarified on 2026-06-24 that the physics-facing material should be understood in the classical natural-philosophy sense: it overlaps with physics, but is not itself a technical physics theory.

Eric also clarified a three-way split that should govern future work:

1. Core physics ontology: reality crystallizes from unconstrained potential into relational hypergraph structure.
2. Signal-processing framing: `c`, `h`, ADC, causal slew rate, and quantization floor are Eric's conceptual framing for the ontology.
3. CHPE thesis: an AI-assisted mathematical/rewrite-system experiment, useful as a probe but not authoritative over the ontology.

That split changes the refinement task. The core natural philosophy does not need to inherit every burden of CHPE. It does need clean definitions, clear registers, and careful contact with established physics.
