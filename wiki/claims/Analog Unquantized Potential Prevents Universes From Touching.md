# Analog Unquantized Potential Prevents Universes From Touching

## Claim

Two universes can never touch because the "space" between them is analog and unquantized, meaning it is infinite relative to any quantized lattice.

## Reasoning

Universes are actualized lattices: quantized relational structures. Between such lattices is not empty spatial distance inside a common container. It is unconstrained analog potential. Because it is unquantized, it is not divided into traversable relational steps. From the perspective of a lattice, that makes the separation effectively infinite.

For two universes to touch, there would need to be a shared quantized relational structure between them. But the between-state is pure analog potential, not space, not distance, and not a bridge.

## Implications

- Separate universes do not collide or touch.
- The separation between universes is not measurable distance but absence of quantized relation.
- Analog potential is infinite relative to a quantized lattice.
- The cosmic horizon is a boundary of actualization, not a surface that can cross into another universe.

## Revision Or Falsification Conditions
