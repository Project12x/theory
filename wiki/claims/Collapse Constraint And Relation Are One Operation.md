# Collapse Constraint And Relation Are One Operation

## Claim

Collapse is one literal operation across levels. Constraint is collapse. Relation is required for constraint. Nothing can be constrained without relation.

## Reasoning

Pure maximum information potential contains no actual information because nothing has been related, differentiated, or constrained. Information begins when potential is constrained into relation: this in relation to that.

`1` in relation to `0` contains information. `1` in relation to infinity also contains information, and once infinity exists in relation to `1`, it is no longer pure unbounded infinity in the same way. Relation constrains. Constraint collapses potential into actual information.

This is not merely an analogy that appears in different domains. The same operation appears at different levels: physical, symbolic, cognitive, social, and cosmological.

## Implications

- Collapse, constraint, and relation should not be treated as separate primitives.
- Pure potential contains no actual information until relation occurs.
- Information is actualized relation.
- Meaning, selfhood, symbolic entities, institutions, and spacetime are downstream forms of the same operation.
- Nothing can be constrained without relation.
