# Ideas Are Physical Brain Patterns

## Claim

An idea is a physical pattern in the brain that matches a symbolic mental constraint. The pattern is poorly understood, but it is physical.

## Reasoning

Ideas do not exist in a separate abstract idea-space. They are instantiated in physical brain activity and structure. A mind experiences an idea as a symbolic or conceptual object, but its existence depends on an embodied pattern that can be maintained, modified, recalled, transmitted, or lost.

This does not reduce away meaning. It grounds meaning. The symbolic mental constraint and the physical brain pattern are two levels of the same instantiated process.

Practice strengthens this dual pattern. Repeated use feeds the symbolic pattern in the mind and the physical pattern in the brain, stabilizing geometric relations across nodes.

## Implications

- Ideas have thermodynamic cost.
- Ideas can disappear if no physical substrate sustains them.
- Shared ideas require transmission between physical brains through physical media.
- Distributed symbolic entities are built from many physically instantiated idea-patterns.
- Practice improves ideas and skills by energetically sustaining and refining their physical-symbolic patterns.
