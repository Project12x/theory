# Truth Of A Claim Is Its Claimed Effect

## Claim

The truth of a claim is whether the effect it claims obtains in physical reality — not whether the operation that carries the claim produces effects in general. A symbolic operation has countless effects; only the claimed effect measures the claim's truth. Truth is therefore a two-place relation between a claim and its designated effect, and it is physical rather than logical: a claim is true when the specific physical effect it picks out occurs.

## Reasoning

Every operation produces many effects. A rain dance synchronizes a group, alters practitioners' brains, burns real energy, and maintains a symbolic entity — all real effects. But the dance's *claim* is rain, and its truth tracks only that. Truth is not in proportion to the magnitude or number of an operation's effects; it is fixed by the one effect the claim designates.

This grounds the real/fake magic falsifiability criterion and states it precisely as two-place: an operation O is real relative to claim C when C's designated effect obtains, and fake relative to C when it does not — regardless of side effects. The same physical operation can be real under one claim and fake under another. Prayer is fake magic as a cancer cure (the claimed effect does not obtain) and real as entity-maintenance (that claimed effect does obtain). The willow-bark remedy is real under "reduces fever" and fake under "appeases the spirit."

Stating truth this way keeps the framework physical-first: truth is the obtaining-or-not of a designated physical effect, not a correspondence relation in a separate logical realm. It also reconciles the criterion with the thesis's own [[The Practice Did Not Change Perception Scale And Success Did]] and its Scale-Does-Not-Validate-Truth argument (source VIII.a): transmission success and adherent count are effects, and effects other than the claimed one never validate a claim.

## Implications

- The real/fake magic criterion is two-place; a single operation carries multiple verdicts under multiple claims. This removes the apparent instability where prayer read as both real and fake — the readings attach to different claims.
- Side effects — cohesion, transmission, scale, energy — never validate a claim. This is the same point as Scale-Does-Not-Validate-Truth (VIII.a), now generalized.
- Practices form the continuous lineage; claims are the mutable layer that selection prunes. Over history, the claims whose designated effects reliably obtain are the ones retained ([[Claims Changed Practices Succeeded]]).
- Truth and value are separated cleanly: truth tracks the claimed effect; value tracks other effects (function, benefit). This complements [[Morality Is A Symbolic Entity Not A Natural Law]].

## Revision Or Falsification Conditions

Revise if truth-evaluation of a claim requires appeal to effects the claim does not designate, or if there is no principled way to fix *which* effect a claim designates. The open work is exactly this last point: specifying how the claimed/designated effect is fixed. Candidate answer under exploration — the effect the practice is maintained under (selection without a selector), which is where this claim meets teleosemantics (see Related Pages).
