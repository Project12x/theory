# Information Boundaries Define Universe Membership

## Claim

Something beyond a pure information boundary cannot be said to actually exist in our universe. It may exist in the larger multiverse, or better, the metaverse.

## Reasoning

In this framework, a universe is a rendered relational information structure. To exist in a universe is to participate in that structure's relations and information boundaries. If something lies beyond a pure information boundary, it is not part of our universe's actualized relational system.

This does not mean it does not exist at all. It means its existence is not in-universe existence. It belongs to the larger structure of possible or actualized universes: the metaverse.

If two lattices do not interact across an information boundary, they do not meaningfully share space. They belong to the same metaverse only in the broader ontological sense, not as neighboring objects in a shared spatial arena.

## Implications

- Event-horizon interiors are not in-universe objects in the ordinary sense.
- The black hole itself can exist in the metaverse while only its horizon exists in our universe.
- Universe membership is informational/relational, not merely spatial.
- "Outside the universe" means outside our relational information structure, not necessarily nonexistence.
- Noninteracting lattices are separated by absence of relation, not merely by distance.

## Revision Or Falsification Conditions
