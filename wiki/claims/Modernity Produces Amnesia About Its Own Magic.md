# Modernity Produces Amnesia About Its Own Magic

## Claim

Most people believe modernity discarded the old magical ways. A small number realize we never did. The continuity is hard to see both because it requires a high level of cognition and because the processes that create change also normalize their own miracles, producing collective amnesia about how impressive they are.

## Reasoning

Once a power becomes reliable, infrastructural, and ordinary, people stop experiencing it as magical. Electric light, wireless communication, powered flight, antibiotics, computation, global navigation, and artificial intelligence become "technology" instead of miracles because they are routinized.

The very success of empirical magic hides it. The more completely a practice works, the more it disappears into the background of ordinary life. The old magical lineage is then misread as discarded superstition rather than as the ancestral field whose successful branches became modern civilization.

Seeing the continuity requires enough symbolic cognition to hold multiple layers at once: old claim, working practice, later recategorization, and present infrastructure.

Physics is one of the clearest examples. Questions that earlier thinkers posed more openly in philosophical or natural-philosophical language about ether, manifestation, causality, and the structure of reality were not abandoned so much as mathematically constrained. As that constrained branch became more precise and successful, modern people forgot that they were still participating in the same deeper symbolic search.

## Implications

- Disenchantment is partly a failure of perception produced by success.
- The ordinary is often only the miraculous after it has been stabilized.
- Symbolic literacy includes the ability to see continuity beneath renamed categories.
- Collective amnesia protects modern people from noticing how strange and powerful their world already is.
- Even physics can forget that it is a constrained descendant of older philosophical and magical inquiry.

## Revision Or Falsification Conditions

This claim would weaken if most people already recognized the continuity between older magical-symbolic practices and modern technology, or if routinized technical success did not reduce perception of wonder, power, and historical continuity.
