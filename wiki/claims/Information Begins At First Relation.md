# Information Begins At First Relation

## Claim

Information begins when the substrate of pure maximum-potential information states is constrained into the first relation: this in relation to that.

## Reasoning

Before relation, there is pure maximum potential: undifferentiated possibility without distinction and without actual information. The first relation creates constraint. Once there is this in relation to that, there is form, difference, and therefore information.

Because time begins with the causal chain of relation, this first relation should not be imagined as occurring after a period of temporal pre-existence. The first relation is also the first moment. See [[There Was No Temporal Before The First Relation]].

Pure potential contains no actual information. `1` in relation to `0` does. `1` in relation to infinity does as well, and once infinity exists in relation to that `1`, it is no longer unconstrained infinity in the same way. Relation constrains; constraint collapses potential into information.

The energy analogy is a heat engine: energy becomes usable only through difference. If the engine and environment are the same temperature, no work can be extracted. Relation is what makes potential usable.

The electrical analogy is current: electrons require a difference across nodes to travel as current.

This makes information prior to cognition. Consciousness can later read, symbolize, and manipulate information, but it does not create information from nothing. It operates on constrained relations that already exist.

## Implications

- Information is fundamentally relational.
- Pure potential contains no actual information until relation constrains it.
- Form is information because form is constrained relation.
- Reading is physical pattern-matching before it is cognition.
- Consciousness is a higher-order symbolic manipulation of already relational information.
