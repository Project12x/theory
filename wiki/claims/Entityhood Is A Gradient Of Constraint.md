# Entityhood Is A Gradient Of Constraint

## Claim

What is and is not an entity is a gradient of constraint, not a binary threshold.

## Reasoning

A symbolic pattern becomes more entity-like as more constraints stabilize it across physical substrates: naming, repeated invocation, thermodynamic investment, peer-to-peer participation, behavioral influence, persistence across minds, self-reproduction, defense, institutional support, evolution, and causal reach.

No single factor creates entityhood by itself. Each one brings the pattern closer to the threshold. A faint idea, a named concept, a fictional character, a god, a nation, a self, an institution, and an AI daemon differ by degree of constraint, stability, energy, participation, and causal force.

At higher levels of stabilization, a pattern can become a low-energy attractor that recruits the gradients needed to keep reinstantiating itself. In those cases, entityhood is not only accumulated constraint but successful self-recurrence.

## Implications

- There is no hard line between idea and entity.
- Gods, nations, cultures, selves, institutions, brands, fictional characters, and AI daemons can be compared by degree of entityhood.
- Symbolic entity power and entityhood are related but not identical: a weak entity may still exist, while a strong one has large causal reach.
- Entityhood is physical, but distributed.
- The question should not be "is it an entity?" but "how entity-like is the pattern, and by what constraints?"
