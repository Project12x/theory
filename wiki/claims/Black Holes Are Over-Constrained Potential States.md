# Black Holes Are Over-Constrained Potential States

## Claim

Black holes, in this system, are functionally identical in structure to our universe. The five facets below are not separate mechanisms; they are one structure viewed from different angles:

1. Where the crystallized lattice is constrained past its stability limit.
2. Where actual information becomes so compressed it functionally returns to pure potential.
3. A boundary between rendered relational structure and unconstrained substrate.
4. A rupture in the lattice that requires extreme energy to create.
5. Not a thing merely inside space, but a place where space stops being able to maintain ordinary relation.

## Reasoning

In the meaning-through-constraint model, constraint extracts usable structure until a [[Resolution Limit]] is reached. Past that point, additional constraint destroys meaningful distinctions.

The black-hole model extends the resolution-limit pattern cosmologically. A region becomes maximally constrained by mass-energy, curvature, information density, and causal boundary. At that extreme, the structured distinctions that make ordinary spacetime legible break down. The region begins to resemble the pre-information state: not empty nothing, but pure potential where ordinary differentiated pattern can no longer be accessed as structured information.

Eric further connects this to the primordial crystal lattice: black holes may represent the energy threshold where the maximum low-energy lattice state breaks or inverts.

This is not a claim that black holes are literally understood by current physics this way. It is the thesis's speculative interpretation of what happens when constraint reaches an extreme.

## Five Facets

### 1. Lattice Stability Limit

The black hole is where the crystallized lattice is constrained beyond what ordinary spacetime relation can maintain.

### 2. Compression Back Into Potential

Actual information becomes so compressed that it stops functioning as ordinary differentiated information and becomes functionally similar to pure potential.

> [!note] Open tension: reabsorption vs unitary recovery (2026-07-01)
> Facet 2 as stated ("reabsorption into potential") reads as information loss, which collides with the post-2019 islands / Page-curve result that information is recovered unitarily in the radiation. Candidate resolution under discussion: holographic complementarity — the interior is encoded on the horizon from our side (information preserved) and a full collapse region from its own side. Adopting it would require reframing facet 2 as *inaccessibility of ordinary structured information from our side* rather than literal return-to-potential. Flagged in [[Open Questions And Term Refinement Register]] and `research/2026-07-01 Natural Philosophy Clarifications.md`; not yet resolved.

### 3. Boundary With Unconstrained Substrate

The event horizon is a phase boundary between rendered relational structure and unconstrained substrate.

### 4. Lattice Rupture

Creating the black hole requires extreme energy because it ruptures or inverts the maximally stable low-energy lattice state.

### 5. Failure Of Ordinary Relation

A black hole is not merely an object inside space. It is a place where space can no longer maintain ordinary relation.

## Recursive Structure

The black-hole structure is functionally identical to the structure of our universe: a rendered relational lattice bounded by unconstrained substrate/potential, with a phase boundary where relation is established or fails. The same structure appears at different scales.

Our universe and a black hole are therefore not merely similar by analogy. They are the same kind of structure in different orientations or scales of the collapse process.

More precisely: the black hole itself does not exist in our universe. Only the event horizon does. The horizon is the in-universe interface; the black hole is the other-side structure of that boundary.

## Implications

- Black holes may be the cosmological version of over-compression past a resolution limit.
- Extreme constraint can become functionally similar to unconstrained potential because usable distinctions collapse.
- The black hole is not merely high information; it is high constraint crossing into a state where information no longer behaves as ordinary structured pattern.
- This connects [[Meaning Increases Through Constraint Until Resolution Limit]] to [[Collapse Cosmology]].
- Black holes may be lattice-breaking or lattice-inverting events.
- Event horizons should be treated as phase boundaries in this model.
- The five facets should be read together, not as competing definitions.
- Black holes and our universe are functionally identical structures in this model.
- Only the event horizon exists in our universe.
- The cosmic horizon and black-hole horizon are the same boundary viewed from different sides.
