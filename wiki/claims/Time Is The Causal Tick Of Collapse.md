# Time Is The Causal Tick Of Collapse

## Claim

Time is not a dimension in the same sense as the three classical spatial dimensions. It is the CPU-cycle tick of the universe: the causal chain of collapse, relation update, and actualization.

## Reasoning

In this framework, space is rendered relational structure. The three classical dimensions map to the positions of stable knot-like patterns on the hypergraph. Time differs in kind. It is not simply another coordinate of the same type. It is the ordered sequence by which collapse proceeds and new relations are established.

This makes time operational rather than container-like. The universe does not move through time as through another spatial axis. Rather, time is what it means for the causal update process to continue. Each tick is a further establishment of relation.

This fits the broader claim that `c` is the primitive causal constant of the emergent universe. If `c` measures the rate of causal update across the minimal relational scale, then time is the sequence produced by that updating, not an independently existing dimension of the same class as spatial extension.

More deeply, this assumes that causality itself emerges from pure causal possibility once collapse begins establishing relation. Time is therefore not prior to the causal chain. It is what the chain looks like as ordered continuation. See [[Causality Emerges From Pure Causal Possibility]].

That also means there is no temporal before in the ordinary sense. The first relation and the first moment are the same thing. See [[There Was No Temporal Before The First Relation]].

## Implications

- Time should be treated as causal sequence rather than as a spatially similar dimension.
- Time dilation reflects changes in relation-update participation, not merely motion through a geometric fourth axis.
- The universe is fundamentally processual: time is produced by collapse rather than presupposed as a container.
- Conscious experience may track collapse from the inside because the self is maintained through ordered symbolic regeneration.

## Revision Or Falsification Conditions

This claim would need revision if time must be treated as fully equivalent in kind to spatial dimensions, or if causal update can be coherently described without an ordered tick/process structure.
