# Noninteracting Lattices Do Not Share Meaningful Space

## Claim

If lattices do not interact, they cannot be said to meaningfully exist in the same space. Lattices can never cross because each expands by actualizing relation at its boundary, not by moving through a shared external container.

## Reasoning

Space is not a neutral container shared by all possible structures. Space is produced by a lattice of constrained relations. If two lattices do not interact, they do not share relational structure, and therefore do not meaningfully occupy the same space.

Each lattice expands by constraining potential at its boundary. The boundary itself is an actualization/information frontier. Separate lattices cannot cross like objects moving through a shared background because there is no shared outside space for them to cross through. They are separated by the absence of shared relation, not by distance inside a common space.

The between-state is analog and unquantized. That means it is infinite relative to any quantized lattice. Without quantized relational steps between them, two universes cannot touch.

## Implications

- Spatial coexistence requires relational interaction.
- Noninteracting universes are not "elsewhere" in ordinary space.
- Lattice boundaries are not traversable surfaces inside a larger room.
- Metaverse structure is relational, not spatial in the ordinary sense.
- Separate universes/lattices can exist without sharing meaningful space.
- Expansion is self-actualization, not motion into an exterior.
- Analog unquantized potential prevents universe-lattices from touching.
