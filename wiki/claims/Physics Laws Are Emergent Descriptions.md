# Physics Laws Are Emergent Descriptions

## Claim

Physical laws describe stable relational patterns produced by collapse rather than prescribing reality from outside.

> [!warning] Needs confirmation
> This is consistent with the philosophy's direction, but its exact strength may reflect AI-organized overstatement. Confirm before treating as settled.

## Reasoning

Under [[Collapse Cosmology]], the universe actualizes into relational structure. Regularities in that structure are what minds later describe as physical law. Mathematics is effective because it describes relational pattern, and physics is relational pattern in actualized form.

## Implications

- Quantum mechanics, classical mechanics, and general relativity may be scale-specific views of the same underlying operation.
- Existing physics frameworks could be partially right while starting too late in the emergence chain.

- Appears in [[The Unbroken Line]].

## Revision Or Falsification Conditions

Needs revision if the framework cannot recover existing successful physics even as scale-limited approximations, or if its predictions contradict established observations without adding explanatory power.
