# There Was No Temporal Before The First Relation

## Claim

There was no temporal "before" the first relation. The first relation and the first moment are the same event.

## Reasoning

If time is the causal tick of collapse, then time does not exist prior to the establishment of relation. It is a category mistake to imagine pure possibility sitting in time for some duration before the first collapse event.

The pre-relational condition is not a waiting room. It has no sequence, no clock, and no elapsed duration. In an infinite medium of pure possibility, all possibilities are present at once. The instability of absolute non-relation therefore does not unfold over time. It resolves immediately at the first moment because the first moment is the first successful relation.

This means the Big Bang should not be imagined as an event that happened after some period of pre-existence. It is the beginning of temporal existence itself.

## Implications

- Pre-existence is not temporal in this framework.
- The Big Bang is the first moment because time begins with first relation.
- Instability of pure possibility should be understood structurally, not as a process unfolding over prior duration.
- The phrase "before the universe" is only meaningful in an ontological sense, not in a temporal one.

## Revision Or Falsification Conditions
