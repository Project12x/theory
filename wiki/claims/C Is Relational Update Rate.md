# C Is The Potential-To-Energy Update Rate

## Claim

`c` is the rate at which pure potential is turned into actual information across the Planck length. Energy may be a related consequence, but information is primary in this definition.

> [!note] Confirmed core claim
> Eric clarified on 2026-04-23 that this is absolutely his belief, not AI residue. He refined it: the speed of light is the rate at which pure potential is turned into actual information across the Planck length. He corrected an intermediate "energy" wording; energy may be true downstream, but information is primary.

## Reasoning

If space is produced as relational structure rather than pre-existing as a container, the speed limit of the universe is the update rate of relational establishment. More specifically, it is the rate at which pure potential becomes actual information across the Planck length.

`c` is mechanical: the rate at which causal relation can update because potential is being converted into information over the minimal relational distance. It helps almost every other pattern and rule exist because causal ordering, differentiation, propagation, interaction, time, and local structure depend on a finite conversion/update rate.

This only makes full sense if causality itself is emergent from a deeper medium of pure causal possibility. In that case, `c` is not just a measured constant inside finished physics; it is the operative rate at which emergent causality begins functioning in the rendered universe. See [[Causality Emerges From Pure Causal Possibility]].

Pure potential contains no usable energy, but contains all potential energy possible. Energy becomes usable only once potential has been constrained into actual information/relations.

The Planck length and `c` are tied to the primordial crystal lattice: the maximally stable low-energy relation-state that sets the minimal relational scale and update rate.

A heat engine illustrates this. It cannot work if the environment is the same temperature as the engine. There may be energy, but without difference there is no usable work. Usable energy requires relation/gradient.

Electric current gives the same pattern: for electrons to travel as current, there must be a difference across nodes.

## Implications

- Time dilation is interpreted operationally as motion approaching the rate at which time-producing relational updates occur.
- Quantization may emerge from finite rendering capacity rather than from a fundamentally discrete substrate.
- Causal order depends on finite update rate.
- Many other patterns and rules in the theory rely on `c` as a mechanical limit.
- Information is actualized potential under finite causal update.
- Energy may become usable downstream of information/relation.
- The Planck length functions as the minimal scale across which this conversion/update is defined.
- The primordial crystal lattice is the structure that sets this scale and rate.
