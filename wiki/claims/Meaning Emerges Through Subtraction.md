# Meaning Emerges Through Subtraction

## Claim

Meaning emerges by constraining possibility. White noise contains too much possibility to be usable; music, language, concepts, and models become meaningful by excluding almost everything else.

> [!note] Refinement
> Constraint can increase usable meaning even as information potential decreases, until a [[Resolution Limit]] is reached.

## Reasoning

Unconstrained possibility is not directly usable. A meaningful pattern is produced when the field of possible forms is reduced into a structure that can be perceived, held, transmitted, or acted upon. Subtraction does not merely remove; it creates usable signal by imposing constraint.

## Implications

- More information is not automatically more meaning.
- Meaning depends on constraint, selection, and exclusion.
- Symbolic systems work because they reduce reality into manipulable patterns.
- Musical tuning systems show this directly: 12-TET and 31-EDO create different musical meanings by constraining pitch space differently.
- Over-constraint eventually destroys meaning by collapsing needed distinctions.
