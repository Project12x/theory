## 1. Onset vs Selection: scoping the low-energy crystallization claim

**[ERIC]** Eric reaffirmed the low-energy crystallization picture: the universe settles into a low-energy state by crystallizing spacetime, like water freezing into ice. This is core (it is [[Primordial Crystal Lattice Is The Maximum Low-Energy State]]).

**[SYNTH]** The distinction that keeps this from re-triggering the fecundity objection: the low-energy/crystallization claim answers **why this structure** (the lattice is the stable low-energy attractor among actualized configurations — why Planck length and `c` come out fixed), not **why collapse at all** (onset). Onset cannot be driven by an energy gradient, because by Eric's own commitment (the 2026-04-23 `c` audit) pure potential "contains no usable energy but all potential energy possible" — so there is no defined energy *across* the void to roll down. Water freezes because ice is lower energy *in a phase diagram that already exists*; before the first relation there is no such landscape. The water metaphor therefore inverts the causal arrow: water is *pulled* by a pre-existing gradient, whereas collapse is *pushed* by the non-persistence of unconstraint (stability **is** constraint; unconstraint cannot persist because there is nothing to hold it) and merely *lands* at low energy.

**Resolution:** keep crystallization/low-energy for structure-selection; keep non-persistence for onset; do not let the energy story do the onset job. So scoped, the fecundity objection does not bite.

## 2. Local update, special relativity, and emergent Lorentz invariance

**[ERIC]** The update is local — there is no global synchronized "now."

**[SYNTH]** This is the special-relativity-compatible camp. It removes the crude conflict (a global update tick would define an absolute simultaneity, which SR forbids) and lets the framework *explain* the relativity of simultaneity: each observer's now-slice is bookkeeping over local updates, so disagreement is expected. Two consequences:

- The source text §XVII.f ("the faster an object moves through space relative to the collapse process...") is the *global-frame* phrasing and gives one-directional dilation. It should be revised to the local reading. (Not edited — source-text change; flagged for a future v16 or errata.)
- Local update is **necessary but not sufficient** for SR. A local rule on a *fixed lattice* still has a detectable preferred frame: the lattice's own rest frame. The real open bar is that the lattice frame be *undetectable* — reciprocal (symmetric) time dilation, i.e. **emergent Lorentz invariance**. This is T2 in mature form.

**[ASSESS]** Nearest equipped neighbor: the analog-gravity / superfluid-vacuum program (Volovik, *The Universe in a Helium Droplet*; Barcelo-Liberati-Visser, *Analogue Gravity*), where an effective Lorentz-invariant world with emergent `c` crystallizes from a non-Lorentzian substrate — Eric's ontology almost verbatim — and which carries the known lethal constraint: emergent Lorentz invariance is generically violated at high energy and must be fine-tuned to survive the ~1e-18 bounds (Collins-Perez-Sudarsky 2004). Candidate mechanism for frame-independence: confluence / causal invariance (CHPE O6/P7). Concrete test: H5 (M0 kernel) checking max-signal-speed invariance under boosts built from graph automorphisms.

## 3. Black-hole mass and holography

**[ERIC]** The black hole's mass "doesn't have to be interior to the black hole as we think of it." This resolves the gravitating-interior contradiction: in GR the mass is not stuff at the center but the mass parameter of the exterior geometry, and on the membrane/holographic reading the degrees of freedom live on the (stretched) horizon. The horizon *is* in-universe ([[Only The Event Horizon Exists In Our Universe]]), so the gravitational relation is with the horizon, not with a severed interior. No contradiction.

**[ERIC]** "Holography has many things we are close up against."

**[SYNTH/ASSESS]** Friendly contacts (lean in): black-hole entropy scaling as *area* (Bekenstein-Hawking) is native to a horizon-carries-everything ontology; the holographic principle (boundary encodes bulk) nearly restates "only the event horizon exists in our universe"; the membrane paradigm places the mass on the stretched horizon; ER=EPR matches entanglement-as-connection. One collision: "reabsorption into potential" (§XVII.e) reads as information *loss*, which conflicts with the post-2019 islands / Page-curve result that information is recovered unitarily in the radiation. **Reconciliation:** complementarity (Susskind) — from our side the interior is holographically encoded on the horizon (information preserved on our books); from its own side it is a full collapse region / child universe; both valid, never simultaneously accessible. Adopting complementarity turns "only the horizon exists in our universe" into a precise holographic-complementarity claim, at the price of retiring/reframing "reabsorption."

## 5. `h` vs the Planck length (open, awaiting naming decision)

**[ERIC]** "`h` is the Planck length, the minimum step `c` can take."

**[ASSESS]** The physical picture is clean — a minimum spatial step, with `c` = step per tick. But the naming has two problems:

- **`h` is the wrong symbol.** `h`/`ħ` is the Planck *constant* (action, joule-seconds); the Planck *length* is `ℓ_P` ≈ 1.6e-35 m. Different quantities, different units. "The minimum step `c` can take" is the Planck length, not `h`.
- **Circularity.** The Planck length is *defined* as `ℓ_P = √(ħG/c³)` — built from the Planck constant, `G`, and `c`. So it cannot be the fundamental primitive that "`h` is"; the length presupposes the constant.

**Recommendation:** posit an own primitive minimum length `λ` and minimum time `τ`, with `c = λ/τ`, and treat `ℓ_P = √(ħG/c³)` as an emergent relationship. In the ADC metaphor, this makes the **spatial-step axis** (`λ`) distinct from the **action-quantum axis** (`ħ`, where amplitude/action quantization and the uncertainty relation actually live) — a cleaner two-axis converter than the current one, in which the spatial step is mislabeled `h`. This also resolves the CSP-vs-§XVII.g inconsistency (whether `h` is independent of or derived from `c`): the spatial step and `c` are linked (one grain), the action quantum is the separate axis. **Awaiting Eric's naming decision** before editing the term register `h` entry and the `c`/quantization pages.
