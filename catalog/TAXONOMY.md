# Taxonomy

This taxonomy is a working map of the philosophical system. Categories can overlap. A single document can belong to several domains.

## Symbolic Operation

The study of symbols as physical constraints that transmit information across minds, time, distance, and substrates.

Key documents:

- [The Unbroken Line](../unbroken_line_thesis_v15.md)

Key concepts:

- Form is information
- Meaning through subtraction
- Empirical magic
- Symbols as range extension
- Ritual as distributed execution

## Consciousness And Selfhood

The view that consciousness begins with a self/not-self distinction: the first cut by which a system recursively models itself as separate from the rest of process.

Key documents:

- [The Unbroken Line](../unbroken_line_thesis_v15.md), especially Section XV

Key concepts:

- Consciousness as the first cut
- Qualia as inside-view collapse
- Self-symbol maintenance
- Compression limits of self-reference

## Civilization And Institutions

The study of religions, moral systems, governments, corporations, fandoms, and artistic canons as symbolic programs running on distributed human networks.

Key documents:

- [The Unbroken Line](../unbroken_line_thesis_v15.md)

Key concepts:

- Gods as functional symbolic entities
- Institutions as distributed virtual machines
- Moral systems as immune response
- Narrative control
- Truth-to-value ratio

## AI And Symbolic Entities

The extension of symbolic philosophy to artificial intelligence as a new substrate for symbolic production and possibly symbolic agency.

Key documents:

- [The Unbroken Line](../unbroken_line_thesis_v15.md), especially Sections III.a and IV

Key concepts:

- AI as symbolic entity
- Taming symbolic influence
- Thermodynamic descent of intelligence
- Human-AI distributed symbolic infrastructure

## Collapse Cosmology

The cosmological claim that reality is the collapse of pure probability into constrained actuality, with space, time, law, matter, and quantization emerging from this process.

Key documents:

- [The Unbroken Line](../unbroken_line_thesis_v15.md), especially Sections XVI and XVII
- [Cosmological Signal Processing](../Cosmological%20Signal%20Processing%20in%20the%20Crystallized%20Probability%20Hypergraph.txt)

Key concepts:

- Analog substrate
- Collapse front
- `c` as relational update rate
- `h` as quantization noise floor
- Black holes as local un-collapse
- Foamy recursive cosmology

## Formal Physics Program

The attempt to translate collapse cosmology into discrete graph-rewriting, testable simulations, mathematical constraints, and correspondence limits with known physics.

Key documents:

- [CHPE](../chpe_thesis_v_1.md)

Key concepts:

- Hypergraph substrate
- Local rewriting
- Path-space amplitudes
- Gauge/isomorphism invariance
- Spectral dimension
- QFT and GR correspondence limits
- Kill criteria
