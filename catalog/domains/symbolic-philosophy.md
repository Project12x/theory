# Symbolic Philosophy

## Scope

This domain covers the claim that symbols are physical arrangements that produce real effects by transferring information across private minds, time, distance, and substrates.

## Source Works

- [The Unbroken Line](../../unbroken_line_thesis_v15.md)

## Central Claims

- Form is information before any mind observes it.
- Symbols are deliberately arranged physical constraints.
- Meaning is produced through subtraction, filtering, and compression.
- "Magic" and "technology" are not separate categories; the real continuity is symbolic manipulation that works.

## Open Questions

- What word, if any, can name the unified symbolic operation without collapsing it into one of its subdomains?
- Can symbolic literacy be taught as a practical discipline?
- What ethical responsibility follows from seeing symbolic systems as operational rather than merely expressive?
