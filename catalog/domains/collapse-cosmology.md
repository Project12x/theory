# Collapse Cosmology

## Scope

This domain covers the cosmological layer: pure probability collapsing into constrained actuality, producing space, time, laws, matter, and quantization.

## Source Works

- [The Unbroken Line](../../unbroken_line_thesis_v15.md), Sections XVI and XVII
- [Cosmological Signal Processing](../../Cosmological%20Signal%20Processing%20in%20the%20Crystallized%20Probability%20Hypergraph.txt)

## Central Claims

- The pre-geometric substrate is analog, unconstrained potential rather than empty space.
- Space is rendered relational structure, not a container.
- Time is the irreversible direction of collapse or relational update.
- `c` is the rate at which new relational information can be established.
- `h` is framed as the effective quantization noise floor of the collapse/rendering process.
- Black holes may be local regions where collapse inverts toward unconstrained substrate.

## Open Questions

- Is the substrate finite or infinite?
- Is `c` exactly constant or only extremely stable across accessible conditions?
- Can dark energy be reframed as the ongoing establishment of relational structure?
- Can black-hole thermodynamics distinguish "hidden information" from "return to possibility"?
