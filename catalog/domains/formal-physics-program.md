# Formal Physics Program

## Scope

This domain covers attempts to operationalize the cosmological philosophy into mathematical rules, simulations, observables, proof obligations, and falsification tests.

## Source Works

- [CHPE](../../chpe_thesis_v_1.md)

## Central Claims

- A universe can be modeled as local rewrites over labeled hypergraph configurations.
- Physical states can be represented in a Hilbert space spanned by admissible configurations.
- Observables should be gauge and graph-isomorphism invariant.
- QFT and GR should emerge as correspondence limits under explicit conditions.
- The theory needs kill criteria, not just explanatory breadth.

## Open Questions

- Can the minimal rule set `M0` pass its spectral-dimension, Lorentz, gauge, fermion, and gravity-proxy tests?
- Can the rewrite amplitudes be made exactly unitary or properly stochastic under clear mode exclusivity?
- Can CHPE recover known physics without importing it by hand?
