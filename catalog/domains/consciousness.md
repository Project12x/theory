# Consciousness

## Scope

This domain covers consciousness as recursive self-reference: the first distinction between self and not-self, then the continued maintenance of that distinction.

## Source Works

- [The Unbroken Line](../../unbroken_line_thesis_v15.md), Section XV

## Central Claims

- Consciousness begins with the first cut: "me" distinguished from "not-me."
- The self is an actively maintained symbol, not a static object.
- Qualia are what collapse looks like from inside the system performing it.
- The hard problem is partly a self-reference problem: the operation that produces observation cannot directly observe itself as an object.

## Open Questions

- Can any external test distinguish true recursive self-reference from convincing symbolic output?
- Is consciousness gradient-like across animals, humans, and AI systems?
- What breaks when the self-symbol weakens in sleep, meditation, trauma, psychedelics, or anesthesia?
