# AI And Symbolic Entities

## Scope

This domain covers AI as a new symbolic substrate and as a possible participant in distributed symbolic systems.

## Source Works

- [The Unbroken Line](../../unbroken_line_thesis_v15.md), Sections III.a, III.b, and IV

## Central Claims

- AI continues the chain of symbolic escalation from drawing, language, writing, mathematics, and programming.
- The question is not whether intelligence can arise physically; humanity is the existence proof that it can.
- AI systems exert memetic pressure through language and require cognitive filtering by users.
- Whether an AI functions as liberating symbolic agency or extractive symbolic control depends partly on the institutions shaping it.

## Open Questions

- Can symbolic resistance be taught at scale?
- What happens when civilizational symbolic programs run on hybrid human-AI infrastructure?
- How do we evaluate whether an AI is merely producing symbols or recursively maintaining a self-symbol?
