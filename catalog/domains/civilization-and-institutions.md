# Civilization And Institutions

## Scope

This domain treats religions, governments, corporations, traditions, fandoms, moral systems, and artistic canons as symbolic structures that persist by running across distributed human nodes.

## Source Works

- [The Unbroken Line](../../unbroken_line_thesis_v15.md)

## Central Claims

- Gods, brands, nations, fictional characters, and equations are all symbolic entities with different truth, value, scale, and transmission properties.
- Institutions preserve symbolic power by controlling narrative, ritual, and evaluation criteria.
- Moral systems can function as structural immune systems, but can also be captured and redirected.
- Traditions are accumulated subtractive work and should be evaluated by current effect, not blindly preserved or discarded.

## Open Questions

- How should a society evaluate symbolic systems without collapsing criticism of institutions into criticism of adherents?
- What distinguishes functional containment from corrupted moral control?
- What vessels can carry difficult symbolic truths past defenses that reject them directly?
