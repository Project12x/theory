# Empirical Magic

## Short Definition

Symbolic manipulation that produces real, observable effects in physical reality.

## Longer Explanation

Empirical magic names the continuity between ancient symbolic practice and modern technology, science, engineering, law, mathematics, art, and AI. The claim is not that fantasy magic works. The claim is that the real historical operation called magic was symbolically mediated causation, and the successful parts were later renamed as specialized disciplines.
