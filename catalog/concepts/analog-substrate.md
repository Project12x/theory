# Analog Substrate

## Short Definition

The pre-geometric field of unconstrained potential from which actualized relational structure emerges.

## Longer Explanation

The analog substrate is not ordinary space, a fluid, or a medium inside spacetime. It is the proposed pre-spatial possibility space prior to rendered relation. In the cosmology layer, collapse converts this unconstrained potential into constrained actual structure. In the signal-processing framing, it is the continuous input that the universe-as-ADC samples into discrete relational output.
