# Relational Update Rate

## Short Definition

The finite rate at which new relational information can be established in the rendered universe, identified speculatively with `c`.

## Longer Explanation

In the collapse cosmology, space is produced relation by relation rather than pre-existing as a container. The speed of light is interpreted as the maximum update rate of this relational rendering process. This reframes `c` as an operational limit: the causal slew rate of the universe rather than merely the speed of light through space.
