# Collapse From Pure Probability

## Short Definition

The operation by which unconstrained possibility becomes constrained actuality.

## Longer Explanation

This is the central operation that links the philosophical and cosmological layers. At the symbolic level, meaning emerges when possibility is reduced into transmissible form. At the consciousness level, selfhood emerges when undifferentiated process is cut into self and not-self. At the cosmological level, spacetime and law emerge when pre-geometric potential collapses into relational structure.
