# Subtraction And Compression

## Thread

Across the repo, meaning and structure emerge by reducing possibility rather than by adding information.

## Appears In

- [The Unbroken Line](../../unbroken_line_thesis_v15.md)

## Pattern

White noise contains maximum possibility but little usable meaning. Music, language, mathematics, selfhood, institutions, and physical law all become stable by discarding most possibilities and preserving a transmissible structure.

## Questions

- When does compression preserve signal, and when does it destroy necessary context?
- Are cultural traditions long-lived compression artifacts?
- Is physical law the earliest stable compression of collapse history?
