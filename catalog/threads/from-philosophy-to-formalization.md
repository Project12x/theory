## Pattern

The philosophical documents generate large-scale claims. The physics-facing documents try to turn those claims into substrate definitions, operators, observables, numerical tests, and falsification criteria.

## Questions

- Which philosophical claims are meant as framing, and which must become mathematically exact?
- Which claims produce predictions that differ from standard physics?
- What is the smallest simulation that could fail usefully?
