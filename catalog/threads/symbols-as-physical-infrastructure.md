# Symbols As Physical Infrastructure

## Thread

Ideas do not travel through an abstract idea-space. They travel through physical media: sound, ink, screens, brains, servers, rituals, habits, and institutions.

## Appears In

- [The Unbroken Line](../../unbroken_line_thesis_v15.md)

## Pattern

Every shared idea requires a physical carrier. This makes symbols fragile, powerful, and operational. If the carrier is destroyed, the symbolic structure can vanish unless copied elsewhere.

## Questions

- How should important symbolic structures be preserved?
- What makes one symbolic carrier more durable than another?
- How does AI change the cost and range of symbolic preservation?
