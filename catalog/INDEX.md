## Maturity Legend

- Fragment: a seed idea, analogy, or unexpanded claim.
- Essay: prose argument with internal coherence.
- Living thesis: large evolving document with multiple linked claims.
- Formalization draft: attempt to make claims operational, mathematical, or testable.
- Research program: includes explicit predictions, tests, proof obligations, or simulations.
